The problem

What pharmacists struggle with every day

Running a pharmacy means balancing too little stock against too much, while the clock runs on every expiry date.

01

Expired medicines

Boxes pass their date unnoticed on a crowded shelf, and the cost is written off.

SmartPharm

The Expiry Monitor groups stock by how soon it expires and flags what to sell, move or return first.

02

Sudden shortages

A fast-selling medicine runs out before the supplier delivers, and the customer leaves empty-handed.

SmartPharm

Days of stock are compared with supplier lead time, so risk shows as Critical or Warning early.

03

Overstock and tied-up cash

Over-ordering “to be safe” locks money in slow movers that may later expire.

SmartPharm

Demand Analysis and the What-If Simulator show how a demand change would affect stock and orders.

04

Finding the right box, fast

Staff lose minutes searching shelves while a patient waits at the counter.

SmartPharm

Search any medicine and see its rack and shelf on the Pharmacy Map. The Prescription Assistant matches a whole prescription.

05

Shelf and system do not match

The count in the software drifts away from what is physically on the shelf.

SmartPharm

Shelf Vision compares expected and detected quantities and shows the difference per medicine.

06

Reordering by gut feeling

Purchase orders depend on memory and manual spreadsheets, and the reasons are rarely recorded.

SmartPharm

The Reorder Center suggests purchase orders and explains what happened, why, and what to do.

How it works

One dataset, one shared state, every page

Every screen reads the same list of medicines, so a number never disagrees with itself between pages.

Dataset

Medicines, stock, sales and expiry from a demo set or an imported CSV.

Processing

Nine derived fields such as days of stock, risk, expiry and stock value.

Shared state

One list of medicines feeds every page.

Rule-based AI

Transparent rules turn numbers into recommended actions.

Decision pages

Dashboard, map, reports and reorder center show the result.

About

A graduation capstone with a practical goal

SmartPharm moves a pharmacy from simply tracking inventory toward intelligent automation. The goal is a tool where a pharmacist can see what needs attention, understand why, and act in a few clicks.

Explainability is central: each recommendation states what happened, why the system flagged it, and what to do next.

Honest scope. Recommendations come from transparent rules on a shared dataset. Prescriptions are read by a real OCR API, but no machine-learning forecasting engine or shelf computer-vision model is running, and no real purchasing takes place.
